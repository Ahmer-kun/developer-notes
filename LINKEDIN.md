## Notes on using this

- Post about one thing you learned, with a link to the note, rather than about the repository in general.
- Only claim what's true. The notes are learning material; don't describe them as production experience unless that's the case.
- The commit history is part of the evidence. Adding notes gradually looks better and is more useful than one large upload.
- No need for metrics, stars, or badges.
